# Iron Ostinato: original procedural music study

68 BPM, approximately 30 seconds. Repeated low strings-like notes, octave bass, heavy offbeat chords, restrained percussion and a sparse second-half upper voice.
Original composition and synthesis; no borrowed melody, audio samples or generative audio model.
This is a timbre/composition sketch, not a realistic orchestra or a seamless loop. Not connected to gameplay.
