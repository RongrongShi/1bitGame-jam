# Procedural pen / brush on paper studies

Original procedural synthesis; no external recordings or AI audio service used.
44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM WAV.
Each preview presents the same 0.30 s, 0.65 s and 1.10 s stroke durations, with gaps.

1. Dry bristles: fine dry brush friction with scattered bristle contacts.
2. Soft ink brush: smoother and darker rubbing, with less grain.
3. Coarse paper scratch: brighter rough paper friction and stronger grain.
4. Heavy ink drag: denser low-mid friction with subdued scraping.

All are sound-design sketches for audition, not recordings of physical writing tools.
The single strokes live in the corresponding subfolders. None are connected to gameplay yet.
