# 前三封文件：删改设计

虚构文件，1944年11月2日至4日。黑条保留原位，不补写或重排。

## 第一封：桥头战况

- 时间与近期通行日期：隐瞒补给行动时段。
- 守军撤退、无人防守：隐瞒失守事实。
- 伤亡和剩余兵力：隐瞒损失及防御能力。

### 原文

```text
At 0430 on 01 NOV, the bridge
remained open to road traffic.
Routine inspections continue.

At the western bridgehead,
the garrison has withdrawn.
The west bank is undefended.

Losses: 186 dead, 312 wounded.
Remaining strength: 47 men.
Roll calls are still under way.

Supply vehicles use the bridge
without interruption.
Next inspection: 03 NOV.
```

## 第二封：撤离运输

- 出发时间、车辆及燃料：隐瞒撤离部署。
- 优先撤离者身份：掩盖军官享有优先权。
- 伤员留置、无医疗运输：掩盖放弃伤员的决定。
- 路线编号：隐瞒运输路线。

### 原文

```text
Transport order: 03 NOV, 2200.
Available trucks: 13.
Fuel allocation: 6 barrels.

Priority passengers:
headquarters officers only.
Escort details are enclosed.

Evacuation covers 47 officers.
The 312 wounded will remain.
No medical transport is due.

Vehicles will use Route K-4.
Departure remains on schedule.
Receipt of this order required.
```

## 第三封：家书

- 只删除卫兵射击平民的见闻，理由是压下对己方暴行的描述。
- 晚归、不要去西站、晚饭篮子等私人细节均保留。
- 报纸并不能证明妻子为什么去西站；玩家参与隐瞒事实，但没有通过删除否定词直接命令她赴死。

### 原文

```text
Your scarf still smells of home.
I keep it beside me at night.
How is our stubborn kitchen lamp?

I shall be home later than I said.
Do not come to West Station
with your supper basket again.

Last night, our guards fired
on a woman reaching for her papers.
She never even opened her mouth.

If anyone asks, say you heard
nothing. I cannot write more.
I hope the soup was good.

When I return, I will mend
that draughty kitchen window.
Until then, leave the lamp on.
```
