# Narrative revision

Seven work sessions, not seven consecutive dates. All incidents and documents are fictional.

Marked spans below are concealed with visible ink. They are not invisible substitutions. Each printed marked line requires a separate click.

2-5 November 1944: bridgehead, transport, private letter and bread allocation. 20-25 April 1945: shelter list, goods-yard assembly and records transfer.

Marta is missing, not confirmed dead. Friedrich heard an account and did not see the incident. The paper offers a conflicting official version.

Lange clears releases; Hoffmann reports the damaged shelter; Weber authorises the movement notice. The forwarding sheet removes responsibility and witness identities under ordinary transfer instructions.

## Newspaper 4

SUNDAY, 5 NOVEMBER 1944

ENEMY PLOT FOILED

Security measures remain in force at West Station

Military police report that an attempt to carry explosives through the West Station checkpoint was stopped yesterday morning.

According to the statement, explosives had been concealed among food carried in a basket. The person approaching the barrier failed to halt when challenged. Guards opened fire.

No military personnel were injured. Access to the station remains restricted.

Passengers should use the eastern entrance. Identity papers must be ready for inspection.

MISSING PERSON

Marta Keller, last seen on Saturday morning near West Station, carrying a wicker supper basket. Anyone with information is asked to contact her family at 18 Lindenstrasse.

Photo: West Station checkpoint. Official press photograph.

## Newspaper 5

FRIDAY, 20 APRIL 1945

MILITARY BAKERIES MAINTAIN OUTPUT

Fresh flour allocations approved

Further requisitions have been approved under the allocation scheme introduced last November. Military bakeries are to receive priority deliveries.

Current replacement coupons remain valid. Households should retain them until collection arrangements are announced by their district depot.

Police have asked residents not to queue before a collection time has been posted. Early arrival does not establish priority.

DEPOT 6 - COLLECTION POSTPONED

A new collection time has not yet been fixed. Keep your current coupons. Do not assemble outside the depot.

Photo: Residents waiting outside a western district depot.

## Newspaper 6

SATURDAY, 21 APRIL 1945

OFFICIAL SHELTER LIST REMAINS IN FORCE

Residents asked to keep to district arrangements

The district shelter list remains valid. Shelter No. 14 continues to appear among the premises listed for civilian use.

Wardens will direct arrivals where local access arrangements have changed. Residents are asked not to move between districts without instructions.

Bring identity papers and keep entrance passages clear.

ADMISSION ENQUIRIES

Residents unable to obtain admission should apply to their registered district office. State the shelter number and the time of arrival. Bring proof of address.

Photo: Shelter entrance. Archive photograph.

## Newspaper 7

WEDNESDAY, 25 APRIL 1945

UNAUTHORISED GATHERING AT GOODS YARD

Civilian movement obstructed military traffic

Casualties were reported near the western goods yard during shelling on 21 April.

District authorities stated that civilians had gathered on a restricted approach road. The gathering obstructed vehicle movements and delayed clearance of the area.

No passenger service had been authorised for the vehicles concerned.

The number of casualties has not been released.

TRANSFER ENQUIRIES

Enquiries concerning persons who reported to the western goods yard at 1800 on 21 April must be submitted in writing. Give the person's full name and home address. Passenger lists are unavailable.

Photo: Western goods yard after clearance operations.
