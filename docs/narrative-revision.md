# Narrative revision

Seven work sessions, not seven consecutive dates. All named incidents and documents are fictional.

Official reports are clearance copies, private letters go to their addressees, and newspapers rewrite cleared material. The player conceals facts; black marks are not invisible edits.

Marta Keller disappears on the morning of 4 November. Friedrich hears the guards' account that morning and writes without knowing the woman's identity. His warning remains intact; the player conceals testimony rather than causing the shooting.

20 April shelter inspection -> 21 April newspaper. 21 April 1600 warning -> 1630 cancellation request -> 1800 assembly -> 25 April newspaper and archive transfer.

SECTOR B-7 / FILE 44-1102
02 NOV 1944 / WESTERN FRONT

Before redaction:

At 0430 on 01 NOV, the bridge remained open to road traffic. Routine inspections continue.

## Work session 3

4 November 1944 / Morning

Before redaction:

Your scarf still smells of home. I keep it beside me at night. How is our stubborn kitchen lamp?

I shall be home later than I said. Do not come to West Station with your supper basket again.

The guards shot a woman at dawn. They said she had reached for her papers at the barrier.

If anyone asks, say you heard nothing. I cannot write more. I hope the soup was good.

When I return, I will mend that draughty kitchen window. Until then, leave the lamp on.

Marked spans: The guards shot a woman at dawn.; They said she had reached; for her papers at the barrier.

## Work session 4

05 NOV 1944 / PUBLIC NOTICE
SUBJECT: BREAD ALLOCATION

Before redaction:

Flour from the western depots is being transferred to military bakeries under the new allocation.

Civilian stocks will last two days. No replacement flour has arrived.

Replacement coupons remain valid. No bread can be issued against them until further notice.

Queues outside Depot 6 were dispersed by police on the orders of District Supply Command.

The public notice will confirm that military production continues and replacement coupons are valid.

Next stock review: 07 NOV. Retain the original inventory.

## Work session 5

AIR-RAID OFFICE / PRESS EXTRACT
20 APR 1945 / INSPECTION REPORT
SUBJECT: SHELTER REGISTER

Before redaction:

Shelter No. 14 remains listed as available in the public register dated 01 FEB.

Its main entrance was destroyed in last night's raid. Civilian admission has stopped.

The annex has a separate entrance. Its cellar is reserved for district officials and families.

Residents were directed to Shelter No. 9 on the next street. It was already full.

The old register is still in use. Replacement entries are attached. This report awaits press clearance.

For the public bulletin, use the entries cleared by this office. Keep the inspection report sealed.

Marked spans: Its main entrance was destroyed; in last night's raid.; Civilian admission has stopped.; district officials and families; It was already full.; Replacement entries are attached.

## Work session 6

DISTRICT COMMAND / PRESS COPY
21 APR 1945 / MOVEMENT NOTICE
SUBJECT: WESTERN GOODS YARD

Before redaction:

Residents awaiting transfer shall assemble at the western goods yard at 1800. Bring identity papers.

Traffic report, 1600: the western passage is under observed fire. The assembly point was hit twice.

Cancellation requested at 1630. No countersigned withdrawal has reached the district office.

Transport allocation: two trucks. Both are assigned to records and command personnel only.

Until a withdrawal is signed, the assembly notice remains posted. Leave military traffic unobstructed.

Retain the signed traffic report. Send only the cleared notice to district boards and the press.

Marked spans: under observed fire; The assembly point was hit twice.; Cancellation requested at 1630.; records; command personnel only
